Torrejas Cubanas

The famous cuban dessert is a mix between a variation of the tradicional French Toast and a traditional Spanish sweet dessert. You can find several different recipes for a Cuban Torrejas along with some other recipes from Honduras, Venezuela, Salvador among other natinalities. However, these are not the same even when they are also delicious in their own way.

Ingredients:

Traditional Recipe commonlly used products.

  • 1 lb of Cuban bread (sweet bread, sobao)
  • 1/2 cup of milk
  • 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract
  • 6 eggs
  • 4 oz of cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup of heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup of guava jam
  • Cinnamon, for sprinkling
  • 4 tablespoons of butter
