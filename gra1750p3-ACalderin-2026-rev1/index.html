THE TASTE SHELTER

TORREJAS CUBANAS

This famous Cuban dessert is a blend of a variation of traditional French toast and a traditional Spanish sweet treat. Several different recipes exist for Cuban Torrejas, as well as other recipes from Honduras, Venezuela, El Salvador, and other countries. However, they are not all the same, although they are all delicious in their own way.

Ingredients:

Traditional recipe ingredients.

For TOAST
  • 1 lb of Cuban bread.
  • 1 cup of whole milk.
  • 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract.
  • 2 eggs yolks.
  • 4 eggs.
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon, for sprinkling.
  • 4 tablespoons of butter or use 3 tablespoon of oil for frying and spread the butter over the toast at the end.

Recipe Variation Ingredients.

  • 4 oz of cream cheese, softened. In this case you should use only ½ cup of whole milk.
  • ½ cup of heavy cream.
  • ½ cup of guava jam.
For SYRUP
  • 1 cup granulated sugar.
  • 1 cup of water.
  • ½ medium lemon peel.
  • 1 cinnamon stick.
  • Honey.
  • Star anise.

Instructions:

To be Prepare.

  • Prepare a shallow bowl for beaten eggs.
  • Prepare a tray where you can place your toasts and another bowl where mix ingredients for syrup.
  • Slice a loaf of bread. Each slice with a half inch thick.
  • Get your bigger saucepan or prepare your oven to a medium low temperature for at least 10 minutes. However, if this is your first time cooking this treat, we recommends you to use the saucepan.

Steps

Making The Syrup
  1. Place into a pot over medium low fire temperature, the cup of sugar and water, the lemon peel and cinnamon stick and simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally until get the syrup-like thick consistency.
  2. After the first 10 minutes of cooking, add the honey, no more of two tablespoon.
  3. When the syrup is ready, add the star anise and let it cool. Then, after the first 15 minutes cooling just remove the peels, the cinnamon stick and the anise.

If you prefer to serve it cold, refrigerate the syrup for at least an hour, but if you want to serve this homemade delicacy warm or at room temperature, simply let the syrup cool for 30 minutes.

For The Toast
  1. Whip the egg yolks until frothy. Add the milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla and mix thoroughly while simming for about 15 minutes to infuse it. Set aside.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat 4 whole eggs until frothy.
  3. Heat a large frying pan. Place butter in the pan—just enough to lightly cover the surface.

Keep all these mixtures separate, ready for the slices of bread.

Making The Toast
  1. Soak each bread slice in the infused milk. Let it get saturated, but not soggy.
  2. Now take the wet bread slice and dip each side in the beaten egg.
  3. Heat the butter in the saucepan or skillet over medium-high heat until it's hot, then fry each slice on both sides until golden.
  4. Fry the coated bread slices in small batches until golden brown on each side. As you make more toast, add a little more butter to the pan to keep the toast from sticking.
  5. Drain the slices on paper towels to remove the excess grease.
  6. Place the fried bread slices in a single layer in a jelly roll or tray and pour the syrup over them slowly so that it gets a chance to soak in.
  7. Before place the tray with your Torrejas in the fridge and above the syrup layer, dust the top of the toasts withh a little cinnamon.
  8. Refrigerate for 1 hour minimum if you want to serve it cold.

Tips:

  1. Torrejas can be stored in the refrigerator for 2 or 3 days. Store them well covered or in an airtight container. They should not be frozen.
  2. For best results, use day-old bread. It will absorb the flavored milk much better than fresh bread, giving the French toast a better texture.
  3. Refrigerate the torrijas for at least 4 hours, or preferably overnight before serving cold.
  4. If using as a hangover cure or strong breakfast, serve with a couple of shots of hot Cuban coffee.
